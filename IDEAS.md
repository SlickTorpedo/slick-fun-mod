# Item ideas

A menu to pick from, not a plan. Nothing here is built yet.

Each entry has a **build cost** so you can weigh what you get against what it takes:

- **S** — a few hours' equivalent, no new infrastructure. Item class, texture, recipe, done.
- **M** — needs a screen, a tick loop, or a mixin into vanilla behaviour.
- **L** — needs a new block with a block entity, a custom entity with a renderer, or
  persistent world data.

Ordered within each section by how much I think your players would actually use them.

---

## Travel and movement

| Item | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Grappling Hook** | Right click a block up to 24 blocks away and get yanked to it. Cooldown, no fall damage on arrival. Enormously fun, and the single best mobility item you could add. | M |
| **Waypoint Stone** | Place one to register a named waypoint; a paired **Recall Charm** teleports you to your most recent one. Consumable charm keeps it honest. | L |
| **Ender Tether** | Right click a player to link; right click again within 60s to swap places with them. Consensual only — they get a prompt. | M |
| **Pocket Boat** | Places a boat under you on water, picks it up when you dismount. Kills the "boat clutter on every shoreline" problem. | S |
| **Slime Boots** | Negates fall damage and bounces you back up a little. Wearable via the same Trinkets path as the collar. | M |
| **Speed Skates** | Big speed boost, but only on ice, packed ice and blue ice. Rewards building ice roads. | S |
| **Updraft Charm** | Consumable puff of air that launches you ~8 blocks straight up. Cheaper and more spammable than the hot dog. | S |
| **Leash Anchor** | Tie any number of leashed mobs to it and carry the lot as one item. Herding animals without the pain. | M |

## Storage and inventory

| Item | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Sorting Wand** | Right click a chest to alphabetise and merge stacks inside it. Absurdly popular on servers. | S |
| **Restock Wand** | Right click a chest to pull matching items from it into your hotbar gaps. Pairs with the sorting wand. | M |
| **Void Filter** | Set a filter list; any listed item you pick up is deleted on the spot. Fixes cobblestone and rotten flesh forever. | M |
| **Vacuum Chest** | A block that hoovers up items in a 6 block radius. The Item Magnet as a placeable. | L |
| **Bundle of Holding** | 54 slots in one item, using the same container component the knife uses. You said you already have backpacks — skip if so. | M |
| **Quiver** | Holds arrows and feeds your bow from a Trinkets slot, freeing an inventory row. | M |

## Building

| Item | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Builder's Wand** | Extends the face you click by up to 8 matching blocks, consuming them from your inventory. The classic, and the most requested building item in any modpack. | M |
| **Exchange Wand** | Swaps blocks of one type for another in a radius. Retexturing a build without breaking it. | M |
| **Scaffold Wand** | Places a temporary platform under you that vanishes on a timer — the Infinite Ladders idea, but horizontal. | M |
| **Plumb Bob** | Shows a coloured beam straight down and marks a grid, for lining up big builds. Client-side rendering. | L |
| **Paint Roller** | Right click any dyeable block to recolour it in place without breaking it. | S |
| **Chisel** | Cycles a block through its variants (stone → cobble → smooth → bricks) in place. | M |

## Information and utility

| Item | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Ore Compass** | Points at the nearest ore of a chosen type within 32 blocks. Set the type by right clicking the ore. | M |
| **Biome Compass** | Points to the nearest instance of a named biome. Uses vanilla's own locate logic. | S |
| **Light Meter** | Shows spawnable blocks near you as coloured particles for 10 seconds. Server-side, no client mod needed. | S |
| **Chunk Border Stick** | Draws the current chunk's borders in particles. Same trick as the light meter. | S |

| **Durability Alarm** | Warns you loudly when any equipped item drops under 10%. Trinkets slot. | S |
| **Tide Clock** | Tells you the exact time, moon phase, and how long until the next phantom night. | S |
| **Spawner Reader** | Right click a spawner to see what it spawns, its cooldown, and whether it's active. | S |

## If you only pick five

1. **Builder's Wand** — the single biggest quality-of-life item for anyone who builds.
2. **Grappling Hook** — the most fun-per-minute thing on this list.
3. **Sorting Wand** — trivial to build, everyone uses it forever.
4. **Harvest Sickle** — saves the most real time of anything here.
5. **Shop Sign** — the only one that changes how players interact with each other.
